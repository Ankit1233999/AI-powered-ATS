# AI-Powered ATS (Applicant Tracking System)

## 📖 Overview

**AI-Powered ATS** is an intelligent, web-based resume evaluation and optimization platform designed to bridge the gap between job seekers and modern automated hiring systems. In today's competitive recruitment landscape, over 75% of resumes are filtered out by Applicant Tracking Systems before ever reaching a human recruiter—often due to missing keywords, poor formatting, or weak alignment with the job description.

This application leverages advanced Artificial Intelligence and Natural Language Processing (NLP) to simulate how real-world ATS algorithms parse, evaluate, and rank resumes. By comparing a candidate's resume directly against a target Job Description (JD), the platform delivers an instant **ATS Compatibility Score**, pinpoints critical **skill and keyword gaps**, and provides **actionable, AI-generated recommendations** to tailor the resume for maximum shortlisting potential.

---

## 🎯 Why This Project?

- **Eliminates Guesswork:** Helps candidates understand exactly how recruiters and automated filters view their resume for a specific role.
- **Saves Time:** Replaces hours of manual keyword hunting with instant, automated gap analysis.
- **Improves Shortlisting Rates:** Guides users on how to rephrase bullet points, highlight relevant technical and soft skills, and align their experience with industry expectations.
- **Accessible & User-Friendly:** Offers a clean, intuitive interface where users can upload their resume and get structured, easy-to-follow feedback within seconds.

---

## ⚙️ How It Works

1. **Upload Resume:** Users upload their resume (in PDF/DOCX format) or paste the raw text directly into the platform.
2. **Input Job Description:** Users paste the complete job description of the role they are targeting.
3. **AI-Driven Analysis:** The backend extracts the text, processes both documents using an AI language model, and evaluates relevance, skill overlap, experience alignment, and keyword density.
4. **Actionable Report:** The dashboard displays a detailed breakdown including the overall match percentage, matched skills, missing keywords, and step-by-step improvement tips.
